# PopMusic

**MUSIC INDUSTRY SIMULATOR**

PopMusic is a 2D music-industry career simulation. You create an artist, write and release songs, hustle the scene week by week, climb the charts against a living roster of NPC stars, sign (or refuse) label deals, buy a lifestyle that actually changes your stats, and eventually run your own imprint.

This is not a licensed-track rhythm campaign with lives and stages. It is an open-ended fame sim: **stamina, cash, chart heat, and reputation** are the resources that decide whether you become a stadium act or vanish into the noise.

Version **1.0.0**. Created and developed by **Nkosinathi Radebe**.

---

## What the game is about

You start as nobody with a cheap mic, **$5,000**, **100 fans**, and an unsigned career. Time begins at **Week 1, Month 1, Year 2025**. There are four weeks in a month and twelve months in a year.

Every week you choose how to spend energy and money: write a single, shoot a video, network, book a bar, drop a diss, pitch a label, rest, or chase a brand deal. Then you tap **Proceed Week**. The industry moves without you if you stall — NPCs release songs, genre heat shifts, rivals climb, royalties land, bills come due, and story events fire.

Success is not a single “win” screen. You measure it in:

- Chart position (Top 30 songs and artists)
- Fan count and weekly listeners
- Cash, royalties, and passive lifestyle income
- Label tier (Unsigned → Indie → Major → Superstar)
- Awards, achievements, and career chapter
- A signed roster of up to five artists on **your** imprint

You can go broke, burn out, get buried by rivals, or stall on the charts. There is no lives bar and no game-over from HP. The pressure is economic, social, and competitive.

---

## The fantasy

Tone is glossy pop-industry drama: leaks, feuds, festivals, ghostwriters, sample lawsuits, brand deals, and awards afterparties.

Your career chapter updates automatically as you grow:

| Chapter | When it appears |
|---|---|
| **Unsigned Hustle** | Unsigned, first ~3 weeks |
| **City Circuit** | Unsigned, weeks 4–11 |
| **Breaking Through** | Unsigned, week 12+ |
| **Indie Rise** | Signed to an indie label |
| **Major League** | Signed to a major |
| **Arena Era** | Superstar label |

A scripted storyline also fires once each as week-end events with real choices. Progress is tracked on the Career screen under **STORYLINE**.

## How a week works

**Proceed Week** (Dashboard app bar) is the heartbeat of the game.

Each advance:

1. Calendar ticks (week 1–4, then the next month; month 12 rolls into a new year and **awards night**).
2. Stamina recovers (about 18–28, helped by discipline).
3. Label stipend and merch pay out.
4. Fan-club upkeep is charged if you founded one. Unpaid dues hurt streams.
5. If you started the week burned out (stamina under 22), you get a **Burned Out** event and a stream penalty.
6. An active tour plays its next city stop.
7. Genre trends shift. Random events and story beats can fire. NPCs release music. Your signed roster acts.
8. Charts recalculate. Royalties pay. Lifestyle assets yield or bill you.
9. A recap dialog opens: **Events**, **Top Charts**, **Player Summary**.
10. The game autosaves (one slot).

If an event needs a decision, the recap shows **Decide first**. Resolve every choice before you close the week.

You can hustle as much as stamina and cash allow **before** you proceed. Doing nothing and tapping Proceed Week still lets the industry move.

## Writing and releasing music

Open **Music → CREATE NEW SONG**, or **Create Music** on the Dashboard.

### Song setup

You choose:

- **Title** (or pick from generated title energy)
- **Genre** (Pop, Rock, Hip-Hop, R&B, Electronic, Indie) — home-genre tracks chart a little hotter
- **Length** — the sweet spot for listeners is about **3.0–3.8 minutes**
- **Studio finish**
- Optional **extra marketing** spend
- Optional **ghostwriter**
- Optional **uncleared sample** (and whether to clear it now)

### Studio finish

| Finish | Pitch | Quality | Viral | Cost vs base | Stamina |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rush mix** | Cheap and fast. Quality takes a hit. | ×0.80 | ×0.90 | −$200 | −8 |
| **Standard** | Normal session. Fair cost, fair mix. | ×1.00 | ×1.00 | $0 | −12 |
| **Polish** | Extra days in the booth. Costs more, sounds bigger. | ×1.18 | ×1.08 | +$900 | −18 |

Base release cost is **$500**, or **$1,000** with extra marketing, plus studio, ghostwriter, and sample-clearance fees.

If stamina is under **30** when you release, quality is cut (about ×0.75) and the game warns you.

The screen shows an **estimated debut rank** (Top 5 through Outside Top 100) before you confirm **RELEASE SONG**.

### Optional minigames (they improve quality; they are skippable)

If you skip them, your artist’s **songwriting** and **production** stats fill in.

**Songwriting minigame** — timed lyric word-pick.

- A category is highlighted (emotion, action, object, nature).
- Tap word chips to build a hook (up to 8 words). Tap a selected chip to remove it.
- Matching the current category builds combo and score.
- Difficulty Easy / Medium / Hard changes the timer, not World Heat.

**Production minigame** — mix matching.

- Drag **Bass / Mid / Treble / Volume** toward moving target values.
- Closer matches score more before the clock runs out.
- The waveform is visual. This is a studio puzzle, not real audio mixing.

Minigames themselves have no soundtrack tied to the track you are making. Chart songs are simulated (title + stats), not playable licensed recordings.

### After release

A song lives in your catalog with quality, viral factor, studio finish, ghostwritten flag, streams, and weekly listeners.

On **Music** you can:

- Browse every released track
- **COMPILE ALBUM** — 3–8 tracks, **$1,500**. Mix your songs with signed-roster tracks. Album cuts get a listener boost
- Clear uncleared samples from a song sheet (uncleared samples can trigger weekly takedown risk)
- Jump toward imprint / roster tools

**Reissue** (Dashboard) lets you revive a catalog cut as a deluxe or remix treatment on a cooldown.

## Performing, tours, and festivals

**Perform** (Activities, drawer, or Dashboard) is how you leave the studio.

### One-off venues

Unlock by popularity and minimum label. You play a **4-lane tap rhythm minigame**, then collect pay and fans.

| Venue | Capacity | Base pay | Unlock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coffee Shop | 30 | $150 | Always |
| Local Bar | 50 | $200 | Always |
| Club | 200 | $800 | 20 popularity |
| Theater | 500 | $2,000 | 35 pop, Indie |
| Concert Hall | 2,000 | $8,000 | 50 pop, Indie |
| Arena | 10,000 | $40,000 | 70 pop, Major |
| Stadium | 50,000 | $200,000 | 85 pop, Superstar |

**Performance minigame**

- Four lane buttons **1–4**
- Hit notes as they enter the green zone (lower part of the screen)
- Combos raise score; misses reset combo and drain **crowd energy**
- Easy / Medium / Hard change duration and note spawn rate (45s / 30s / 20s)

### Tours

Book a multi-week run. Each **Proceed Week** plays the next city (Austin, Nashville, Chicago, Brooklyn, Atlanta, Seattle, Miami, Denver, London, Tokyo), pays door + merch, adds fans, and costs stamina.

| Tour | Weeks | Pay / stop | Merch / stop | Fans / stop | Stamina / stop | Unlock |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bar Crawl | 2 | $420 | $80 | 40 | −14 | Open |
| Club Circuit | 3 | $1,100 | $220 | 120 | −18 | 20 pop |
| Theater Run | 3 | $2,800 | $480 | 280 | −20 | 35 pop, Indie |
| Arena Tour | 4 | $18,000 | $2,200 | 1,800 | −24 | 70 pop, Major |
| Stadium Run | 4 | $90,000 | $9,000 | 8,000 | −28 | 85 pop, Superstar |

### Festival

One exclusive summer slot, **June–August**, once per in-game year. Heat is up on Pop / Electronic / Hip-Hop during festival season. Missing the window means waiting until next summer.

## Charts

**Charts → Top 30**. Two views: **Songs** and **Artists**.

Filter by **All**, **New Releases**, or a specific genre. The header shows biggest gainer / dropper energy and a live snapshot note: ranks update from quality, promo, and when the week proceeds.

A song’s chart score blends weekly listeners, lifetime streams, recency, quality, and viral heat. Weekly listeners scale with:

- Your popularity, fan connection, and reputation
- Recency (debut week is especially hot)
- Song quality and viral factor from writing / production / studio / marketing
- Length sweet spot
- Genre heat and the current trending genre
- Home-genre bonus
- Album, video, and playlist flags
- Rival pressure, festival season, press cover, fan club, street team
- Burnout / low stamina penalties

Royalties are paid from weekly listeners × your **stream cut** (label deal). Unsigned keeps **100%**. Indie keeps **82%**. Major keeps **55%**. Superstar keeps **70%**. Deal style (Standard / 360 Advance / Artist-owned) shifts keep %, stipend, and signing check.

Chart events fire when you enter the Top 30, climb, or hit **#1**.

## Career, labels, and story

**Career** is your trophy room and promotion desk.

### Label progression (must climb in order)

| Tier | Requirements | Weekly stipend | Artist stream keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Unsigned** | Starting | $200 | 100% |
| **Indie** | 25 popularity, 5 songs | $800 | 82% |
| **Major** | 60 popularity, 50K fans, 1 award | $2,500 | 55% |
| **Superstar** | 85 popularity, 500K fans, 3 awards | $8,000 | 70% |

When you sign up a tier you pick a **deal style**:

| Deal | Pitch |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Normal stipend and stream split |
| **360 / Advance** | Huge signing check and fatter weekly check. Label keeps more streams |
| **Artist-owned** | Tiny advance, thinner stipend, you keep more of every stream |

Indie signing advance is on the order of **$2,500** (standard), majors **$18,000**, superstar **$80,000**, scaled by deal style.

### World labels you can pitch

Pitching costs money, has a **4-week cooldown**, and can be rejected (**6-week** cool-off). Each house has a city, CEO, and focus.

| Label | Tier | City | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cassette Heart | Indie | Portland | Tape-first DIY, all-ages rooms |
| Harbor Lights | Indie | Brighton | Coastal indie pop, playlists over billboards |
| Neon Alley | Indie | Seoul | Night-drive electronic, club residencies |
| Apex Sound | Major | Los Angeles | Radio, video budgets, long legal team |
| Goldline Media | Major | New York | Brand deals bundled with the memo |
| Voltwave | Major | London | Transatlantic festivals and EU/UK tours |
| Crownworld | Superstar | Los Angeles | Stadiums. They only take what already moves units |
| Infinity House | Superstar | Tokyo | Slow inbox, huge machines, world tours |

### Storyline beats (Career → STORYLINE)

Each fires **once** as a week-end event:

1. **Unsigned Hustle** — “You are nobody with a dream and a cheap mic.” *Write Tonight / Network Instead / Rest & Plan*
2. **Debut Single** — first song is out. *Push Promo Hard / Start the Next Track / Play Every Bar*
3. **The Clip (Scandal)** — an old argument video surfaces. *Own It / Lawyer Up / Flood the Feed*
4. **Indie Offer** — unsigned, ~22 popularity, 2+ songs. Neon Harbor wants you. *Take the Meeting / Hold Out / Leak the Interest*
5. **Rival Feud** — a rival claims you copied their sound. *Diss Track Energy / Stay Classy / Collab to End It*
6. **Festival Breakthrough** — second-stage slot. *Take the Slot / Negotiate Pay / Pass for Studio Time*
7. **Viral Season** — a track hits serious streams or viral heat. *Ride the Wave / Brand Deal / Protect the Art*
8. **Major Label Fork** — Indie + ~55 popularity. Two majors call. *Chase the Stadiums / Artist-Friendly Deal / Stay Indie Power*

Year-end brings **awards night** and afterparty choices (*Work the Room / Go Nuclear / Slip Out Early*).

### Year-end awards

- Song of the Year
- Artist of the Year
- Breakthrough Artist

Winning pays prestige (and Breakthrough includes a cash prize). Awards also gate the Major and Superstar label tiers.

## Your imprint — become the label

Once you are in the industry, **Record Labels** includes **`[Your Name] Records`**.

You are Founder & CEO. You can:

- Sign up to **5** NPC artists to the roster
- Negotiate a **custom royalty split** on the Signing Contract screen (artists have a minimum keep % they will accept)
- **Commission a demo** from signed talent
- Restyle / rename roster artists
- Run CEO ops on a **3-week cooldown** per action:
  - **Book a gig** — a performance that fits their heat
  - **Playlist push** — editorial bump for recent releases
  - **Press day** — interviews that lift reputation
  - **Studio block** — paid studio time to sharpen the next release

Signed artists write and release on your watch. You pick when their work hits. Your cut of their streams is a real income line.

From an artist’s profile you can also **Request Collab ($400)** even if they are not signed to you.

## XP and leveling

Hustles, releases (~50 XP), albums (~80 XP), money, and fans feed a level bar. The curve grows with level (on the order of `100 × level² × 0.5` XP per level). Level is prestige and challenge fuel; unlocks are mostly popularity / label / awards, not raw level gates.

## How to play well (without spoiling the sim)

1. **Write something in the first weeks.** The debut story beat and First Hit achievement both wait on a release.
2. **Watch stamina.** Releasing tired tanks quality. Starting a week under 22 stamina burns streams.
3. **Promo is half the chart.** A decent song with a video, radio pitch, and home-genre timing beats a perfect mix you never push.
4. **World Heat Hard is a crowded chart, not a damage modifier.** You still write the same songs; NPCs just hit harder.
5. **Label deals are a trade.** Majors pay and open arenas while taking a bigger stream cut. Artist-owned keeps the art and the money per stream.
6. **Do not ignore upkeep.** Fan club, luxury, and investments can print money or sink a cold week.
7. **Rivals are content.** Diss, truce, or collab — all three are valid careers.
8. **Festival is seasonal.** If it is not June–August, the slot is gone until next year.
9. **The imprint is the late game.** Five artists, custom splits, CEO actions — that is how a star becomes a house.
10. **Proceed Week is the only clock that matters.** Hustle first, then let the industry resolve.
